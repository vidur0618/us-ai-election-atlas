- NCSL misidentifies the New York moratorium bill pair as passed; the official A10141A remains in committee. The atlas already correctly separates it from S10642/A11560.
- SSRS Table1 and Table3 give different GA overall sample totals(4198 versus4146). The existing data follows the margin-of-error table. Keep that table provenance; neither is a question-specific sample.

- EVP Google Sheets toplines and crosstabs linked from its report were inaccessible. The questionnaire-level bases and exact full wording remain unresolved.
- Grouped policy rows are not individual-bill counts. California’s seven-bill package has summary-level support; several paired bills were checked through one companion or session summary.
